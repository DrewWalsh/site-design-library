# Experimental directions 76–100

Research conducted 26 October 2026. The expansion explores twenty-five navigation mechanisms, not twenty-five decorative skins. Each specimen contains an authored body of material, a substantial detail state and a complete reading or collection journey. The original fifty and commercial/institutional additions remain separate references.

## Primary precedents

- [Conditional Design manifesto](https://conditionaldesign.org/manifesto/): Explicit rules can make participation and form intelligible.
- [Moniker — Conditional Design Workbook](https://studiomoniker.com/projects/conditional-design-workbook): A constrained process can support a complete participatory publication.
- [Moniker — Click Click Click](https://studiomoniker.com/projects/click-click-click): Intentional browser actions can become visible narrative material.
- [Moniker — Neuhaus](https://studiomoniker.com/projects/neuhaus): A fixed composition can hold independently changing contributions.
- [Typatone](https://typatone.com/): Text and a sequence of actions can also work as a score.
- [Nicky Case — The Wisdom and/or Madness of Crowds](https://ncase.me/crowds/): Controls, consequences and explanations can form one coherent argument.
- [Zach Lieberman / Data Arts Team — Land Lines](https://experiments.withgoogle.com/land-lines): A gesture can retrieve existing material through a shared visual structure.
- [Rafaël Rozendaal — Internet works](https://www.newrafael.com/internet?filter=website): The browser surface itself can be the composition.
- [Radio Garden — About](https://radio.garden/settings/radio-garden): A physical selection metaphor can organise a large collection without eliminating direct access.
- [Neal Agarwal — The Deep Sea](https://neal.fun/deep-sea/): One meaningful dimension can organise a substantial body of content.
- [Aaron Koblin — The Wilderness Downtown](https://www.aaronkoblin.com/project/the-wilderness-downtown/): The browser can participate in the structure of a narrative.
- [Bruno Simon — HTML version](https://bruno-simon.com/html/): Unusual primary navigation can coexist with a direct readable route.

These sources informed interaction principles. No external artwork, source code, brands, prose or page layouts were copied. All new illustrations are original SVG/CSS geometry. The small image vocabulary is deliberate here: a functioning fold, score, object, silhouette or scale transition carries the idea more clearly than a stock photograph.

## Boundaries that keep experimentation useful

- Every spatial control has a native button or input; drawing, sound and playback are optional. Gesture Index explains its small deterministic classifier rather than claiming general image recognition.
- Reduced-motion preferences remove transitions. Time-based specimens do not start automatically and provide pause, frame/measure selection and complete text.
- Each composition includes a direct index, ancestry, transcript or finite route. Unusual discovery does not make information permanently inaccessible.
- Phone layouts preserve the organising idea while adapting its geometry: a sheet becomes an accordion, a split essay stacks by speaker, a score uses a bounded scroller, and semantic zoom remains discrete.
- Demo content is original fiction. Causal Theatre states the assumptions of its toy model; Inside the Object does not present a schematic interaction as real repair advice.
- Each ZIP preserves source, local runtime assets, four reference images, capture provenance, licenses and an adaptation prompt.
