# Collection diversity review — 29 October 2026

All 100 packages were reviewed through their source, specifications, metadata, tokens and existing desktop, phone and full-page captures. The revision changes 58 examples: 12 of 01–30, 14 of 31–50, seven of 51–75, and all 25 experimental examples. Stable IDs, routes and source directories are preserved.

## What was repeating

1. **The same opening argument.** A narrow masthead, eyebrow, large left-aligned two-line proposition and small explanatory paragraph frequently appeared before the actual object, tool, story or choice. Different colours and subjects did not change that structure.
2. **A shared scale and rhythm.** Repeated heading clamps, 1240px base widths, 65ch text measures and eight-pixel spacing defaults contributed to similar proportions. Ruled sections, title-left/content-right layouts, coloured bands and final calls to action repeated below the opening. These defaults were most noticeable where the composition did little to override them.
3. **Experimental interaction presented as conventional marketing.** A map, score, drawing field, cinema timeline or stage was often introduced like a product feature. Its promised navigation occupied a smaller region below the same introductory headline.
4. **Playfulness concentrated in decoration.** Several playful pages used conventional introduction-and-grid structures with colourful shapes placed inside them. The shapes now organise navigation, scale and content in the affected examples.
5. **Phone layouts repeated the introduction first.** Stacking the generic headline and paragraph pushed staff, capacities, floorplans, urgent choices and vacancies below the first screen. Some desktop metaphors also became undifferentiated blocks when stacked.
6. **Repetition inside examples.** The graphic index reused six drawings across 24 entries. It now has 24 distinct compositions and density settings with finite pagination. The comic and machine examples now have fuller original scenes and physical context.

## What changed

The experimental group now opens with its actual medium: folded paper, an atlas, an aperture, a tuning dial, braided text, opposing fields, strata, branching prose, a causal machine, a redacted dossier, a printing press, an exploded object, recursive regions, negative space, a folded ribbon, patch cables, an action disc, a drawing field, a score, a film frame, a rule board, a curtain, shadows, an assembled landscape and a scale journey. These are different compositions and navigation models, not palette variants. Supporting routes retain the relevant vocabulary and expose the same content through native controls.

The playful group now includes a geometric programme poster, shaped parcels, a tactile circuit bench, a hand-drawn lesson notebook, an accession cabinet, comic panels, a file browser, a broadcast index, a dated workshop board, a typographic performance, adjoining colour planes, a museum room, a pattern instrument and a dense graphic collection.

Selected conventional examples start with their actual subject or decision: conference programme, project plate, documentary evidence, magazine cover, records, map, plot, directory, market stalls, menu, field report, hotel arrival, staff availability, storage inventory, nursery day, venue arrangement, dispatch choice, contribution allocation and vacancies. Practical navigation is retained where it helps a business or task.

## Measured opening geometry

A repeatable browser pass inspected all 100 entry pages at 1440 × 1000 with loaded fonts. A “top masthead” means the first header is visible, above y=20, wider than 1100px and shorter than 150px. A “large upper-left title” means the first h1 is visible, left of x=250, above y=300 and larger than 45px.

| Indicator | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| Broad, shallow top masthead | 91 | 60 |
| Large upper-left opening title | 65 | 26 |

These are descriptive counts, not a design-quality score. A masthead or headline can be correct for a particular example. The pass records only the first matching element; it does not assess contrast, semantics or artistic merit. Visual review of complete pages and phone adaptations determines whether the composition works.

## Reference integrity and future additions

The revised packages carry composition-specific briefs, metadata and new desktop, phone, supporting-state and full-page captures. ZIPs include the revised source and captures. Read the scoped composition rules in Demo.vue together with the base tokens; token values alone do not describe the canvas.

For a new example, decide what the visitor actually encounters first and how that object organises navigation. Compare the opening, full-page rhythm and phone sequence with its nearest neighbours. Reuse native controls and accessibility helpers freely, but avoid inheriting a page composition by default. Keep a useful conventional example when its audience benefits from familiarity. Experimental navigation must still expose usable controls, legible content, a return path and a reduced-motion experience.

Verification combines source/type/build validation, four-width reflow checks, keyboard entry, direct supporting routes, automated accessibility checks and interaction journeys. Automated accessibility checks supplement visual review; they are not a claim of formal certification.
