# Reuse and asset notices

The original source code, design specifications, sample writing and original vector artwork are available under the MIT license in `LICENSE`. Use and adapt them for personal, client and commercial projects. Keep the copyright and license notice in substantial copies; a visible site credit is not required.

The project makes its generated illustrations available on the same terms, to the extent rights exist. They depict fictional people and settings and must not be presented as photographs of actual staff, clients or completed work.

Third-party photographs and fonts retain their own terms. They are not relicensed under MIT:

- **Unsplash photographs:** the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) permits free commercial and noncommercial use and distribution. It prohibits selling images without significant modification and compiling a competing image service. Retain the source and creator information in the asset manifests. The images illustrate these designed examples; they are not evidence of a real business or an endorsement by a pictured person.
- **Fonts:** the bundled families use SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their complete copyright and license notices accompany the font files in `public/fonts/*-OFL.txt`. Preserve those notices when redistributing the fonts.
- **Dependencies:** Vue, Vite and other installed packages retain their respective licenses, distributed with those packages. The downloads contain source and assets, not `node_modules`.
- **Reference screenshots:** these document the original layouts and can include the photographs and fonts above. The same asset notices apply; the screenshots are not a separate stock-image collection.

See `docs/ASSETS.md`, `docs/COMMERCIAL_ASSETS.md` and the machine-readable manifests in `public/art/` and `public/fonts/`. Each individual ZIP includes the relevant asset records, font notices, screenshots and this file. Research links acknowledge influences; artwork, code and branding from those external websites are not copied into the library.

Replace fictional names, prices, availability, qualifications and claims with verified project content. The interactions are local demonstrations; no bookings, payments, applications or messages are sent.
