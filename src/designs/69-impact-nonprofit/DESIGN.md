# 69 Impact Nonprofit

**Philosophy and fit.** Connect a contribution to a specific, accountable project. Built for nonprofit, charity, environment. The visitor’s next step is to choose a contribution.

**Visual grammar.** Use a earthy campaign publication with project-led contribution allocation. Preserve the hierarchy of project outcomes, use of funds, contribution choices; these patterns solve this business’s actual decision, rather than supplying interchangeable decoration.

**Composition.** A measured 120-metre planting target and a graphic bank transect open the nonprofit. An allocation receipt reacts to contribution amounts alongside it. Project records, the river diagram and volunteering follow, with planned outcomes clearly distinguished from completed results.

**Sample and interaction.** Common Ground / River trust is a fictional demonstration. The entry page establishes a complete visual direction, with choose your contribution and project outcomes as supporting states. Inputs use local deterministic fixtures; no request, order, application or payment is sent.

**Responsive and inclusive behaviour.** The target and transect come first, then the contribution receipt. Allocation figures retain their labels and update without motion. Native buttons, visible focus, a direct return path and reduced-motion support preserve access to the same content.

Use imagery to explain the place, people, work or product, with deliberate crops and honest captions. Do not turn this into a generic hero-and-card layout.
