# 91 Patchbay Editions

**Idea.** A publication can emerge from connecting a subject to a method.

**Composition.** A physical patch console fills the opening: two banks of large metal sockets flank a recessed editorial display, with heavy cables crossing the panel. Subjects and methods are the navigation. The small chassis label replaces the former introductory headline.

**Navigation and complete example.** Connect one of three subjects to one of three methods, discover nine original micro-essays, read the pairing rationale and save a local patch sheet.

**Responsive and inclusive behaviour.** The phone console stacks a row of subject sockets, the connected essay, and a row of method sockets. All nine authored connections remain selectable without dragging. Native buttons, visible focus, a direct return path and reduced-motion support preserve access to the same content.

**Adaptation.** Keep the grammar small enough to understand and ensure every allowed connection yields deliberate content. Do not simulate arbitrary generation. Retain the relationship between navigation and content. Do not reduce the reference to a generic hero followed by interchangeable cards.

**Research.** [Conditional Design manifesto](https://conditionaldesign.org/manifesto/); [Moniker — Conditional Design Workbook](https://studiomoniker.com/projects/conditional-design-workbook). These are conceptual precedents, not copied artwork, source code, branding or text. All diagrams and sample writing in this specimen are original, fictional material.
