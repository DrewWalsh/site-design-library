**Philosophy and fit.** Tell the year’s story and show the evidence behind it. Built for public organisation, nonprofit, annual reporting. The visitor’s next step is to explore the report.

**Visual grammar.** Use a burgundy editorial report with oversized year numerals and clearly labelled financial tables. Preserve the hierarchy of achievements, financial data, project outcomes, sources; these patterns solve this business’s actual decision, rather than supplying interchangeable decoration.

**Sample and interaction.** Northbank / Public library is a fictional demonstration.
