# 79 Frequency House

**Idea.** Tune a publication instead of moving through a menu.

**Composition.** One overscale analogue tuning dial is the opening composition. Calibration rings surround the programme at its centre; a circular sequence label and a low pill-shaped tuner are separate instrument parts, with no masthead strip.

**Navigation and complete example.** Tune six fictional late-night programmes, read their transcripts and liner notes, and save an ordered listening sequence. This silent specimen demonstrates tuning without a live radio dependency.

**Responsive and inclusive behaviour.** The dial remains circular on phones; explicit station buttons, a range and next/previous tuning controls duplicate the spatial interaction. Native buttons, visible focus, a direct return path and reduced-motion support preserve access to the same content.

**Adaptation.** Use meaningful frequency stops and programme content inside the instrument. Keep listening optional and offer complete transcripts. Retain the relationship between navigation and content. Do not reduce the reference to a generic hero followed by interchangeable cards.

**Research.** [Radio Garden — About](https://radio.garden/settings/radio-garden); [Typatone](https://typatone.com/). These are conceptual precedents, not copied artwork, source code, branding or text. All diagrams and sample writing in this specimen are original, fictional material.
