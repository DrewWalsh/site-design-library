# 52 Corner Shop

**Philosophy and fit.** Make a small shop feel like part of its neighbourhood. Built for independent retail, gift shop, local shopping. The visitor’s next step is to reserve for collection.

**Visual grammar.** Use a warm yellow shop window with stacked notices and a stock shelf. Preserve the hierarchy of opening hours, featured stock, local pickup; these patterns solve this business’s actual decision, rather than supplying interchangeable decoration.

**Sample and interaction.** Good Neighbour is a fictional demonstration. The entry page establishes a complete visual direction, with collection basket and collection details as supporting states. Inputs use local deterministic fixtures; no request, order, application or payment is sent.

**Responsive behaviour.** On a phone, keep the primary decision first, stack facts with explicit labels, preserve the order of the workflow, and provide comfortably sized controls. Wide diagrams scale; tabular facts retain their labels.
